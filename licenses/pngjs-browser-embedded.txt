Modules embedded in pngjs/browser.js

pngjs (bundled into this package) ships browser.js, a browserify 17.0.0 build that
embeds the following Node.js compatibility modules. Their license texts are not
present in the pngjs package, so they are reproduced here.

Versions are those resolved from the browserify@17.0.0 dependency tree and may
differ from the exact versions embedded in pngjs/browser.js. Where several
versions were resolved, their license texts were identical.
